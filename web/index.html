Impulse and Leq are not published in the bundle — they are computed here from the full-rate samples by the same method the report uses. The shaded stretch is what each figure was taken from: the impulse is the largest the running integral gets before the trough, not where it ends up, and the Leq peak is looked for in the 25 ms after the shot starts.

One-third-octave energy, divided by each band's width. Bands widen with frequency, so plotting them raw slopes up about 1 dB per band no matter what the sound is; per Hz is the honest shape. A-weighting discounts everything below about 250 Hz, so two cans can share a dBA figure and not sound alike.
